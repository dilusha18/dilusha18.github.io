I'm a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Moratuwa with a strong interest in product development, robotics, mechatronics, industrial automation, and R&D. I enjoy turning engineering ideas into practical systems, from early concepts and CAD models to automated systems, prototypes, testing, and functional products.

My core focus is mechanical design and product development, with hands-on experience in SolidWorks, AutoCAD, mechanism design, mechanical assemblies, rapid prototyping, and basic FEA/CFD. Through academic and innovation projects, I have worked on autonomous robots, UAV mechanisms, wearable IoT devices, gearbox design, and reverse-engineered products.

Alongside mechanical engineering, I have experience with embedded systems, PCB design, sensors, ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Python, and C++. This multidisciplinary background allows me to work comfortably at the intersection of mechanics, electronics, and software.

Machine Design Project

Fish Feed Extruder Gearbox

Designing a multi-speed gearbox with three forward speeds and reverse operation for an industrial fish-feed extruder. My detailed work covers flange couplings, lubrication selection, bearing calculations, and the internal bearing arrangement.

  • Gearbox Design

Mechanism Design

UAV Self-Leveling Landing Gear

A passive mechanical landing gear system designed to enable Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) to securely touch down on unstructured and uneven terrain. By leveraging intelligent kinematic design, this approach achieves autonomous self-leveling without the computational overhead of complex, active sensor-based control systems.

  • Mechanism Design
  • MATLAB
  • Optimization
